01 — Capture

It starts with light.

Every day, enormous amounts of solar energy reach your rooftop. Ellipse Solar designs systems engineered to capture that energy efficiently.

Array

From light to current.

The array resolves as the photons arrive. Each cell answers with a small electrical pulse.

Generation

Light becomes electricity.

Photovoltaic cells convert incoming light into direct current. The array is designed around the roof, the shade, and the way the property actually uses energy.

  • High-efficiency solar modules
  • 25+ year performance life
  • Engineered system design

02 — Convert

Power, intelligently converted.

Direct current from the modules passes through the inverter and leaves as alternating current — the form of electricity the property already runs on.

DC power → Inverter → AC power
