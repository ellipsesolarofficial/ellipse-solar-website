Our Services

Solar Solutions for Every Need

The same systems our quotation tool prices: on-grid, off-grid, and hybrid, from 3 kW to 50 kW.

On-Grid Solar

Tied to your DISCOM connection. Daytime load runs on solar, and surplus units are exported. Available in 1-phase and 3-phase, with Polycab, Solaire, or Xwatt inverters.

Get Started →

Off-Grid Solar

For sites that need power without depending on the grid. Polycab off-grid inverters with a lead-acid or lithium (LiFePO4) battery bank.

Get Started →

Hybrid Solar

Grid plus battery. The system exports surplus when the grid is up and keeps essential loads running during an outage.

Get Started →

Rooftop & Ground Mount

Standard rooftop, difficult-access sites, or open ground mounting. Structures in TATA Structura or Apollo GI, for buildings up to 10 floors.

Equipment

Brands We Install

Taken from the catalogue our sales team uses to build every quotation.

Solar panels

MonoPERC bifacial and TOPCon bifacial modules, in DCR (required for subsidy) or non-DCR. Wattage is matched to the brand, typically 540W–630W.

  • INA
  • Adani
  • Waaree
  • Tata
  • Eastman
  • Luminous
  • Panasonic
  • Havells
  • Reliance
  • Renewsys
  • Premier
Performance warranty: 25 years. INA, Renewsys, and Premier: 30 years.

Inverters

On-grid and hybrid options in 1-phase and 3-phase. Off-grid systems use Polycab. Tata Power Solar kits can also ship with Eastman, Goodwe, Solis, Sofar, Solax, or Foxx inverters.

  • Polycab
  • Solaire
  • Xwatt
On-grid warranty: Polycab 8 years, Solaire and Xwatt 10 years. Hybrid: Polycab 8 years, Solaire and Xwatt 5 years.

Batteries

Optional on on-grid, required for off-grid and hybrid. Lead-acid for standard backup, lithium iron phosphate for longer life and higher usable capacity.

  • Exide
  • Luminous
  • Livguard
  • Amptek
  • Solaire
Lead-acid (Exide, Luminous, Livguard) and Amptek lithium: 5 years. Solaire lithium: 10 years.

Mounting structure

Galvanised structures for rooftops and ground mounts, sized with the DC and earthing cable run for the number of floors.

  • TATA Structura
  • Apollo
Includes ACDB/DCDB, earthing, lightning arrestor, and solar meter where the site does not already have one.
