## Priority Issues

### [P0] Story frames draw on top of their own headlines

On the phone home beat, “Sunlight. Now powering your home.” runs through “Imagine producing as much energy as you consume.” and the illustrative disclaimer. On desktop, the house line drawing cuts through that headline and through “Use what you need. Send the rest forward.” In “Why Ellipse Solar,” two statements sit in the same box and crossfade into “Endteterm” and “endrexecution.”

**Why it matters:** This is the middle of the sales story. If the bill drop and the reasons to trust Ellipse cannot be read, the scroll has no payoff.

**Fix:** One text block per frame. On small screens, put the meter in a band that does not share the headline’s box. In the statements, show only the active sentence.

**Suggested command:** /impeccable layout

### [P1] “Calculate my solar savings” does not calculate

The button is a link to `#contact`. At ₹3000, at a cleared field, and at ₹200, the result stays 3 kW, 5,639 units, and ₹78,000. “Yes — 100 units free” changes nothing at ₹3000 and changes the system at ₹8000, with no explanation. “Up to ₹17,000 in state subsidy” never appears in the results. Typing ₹90,000 leaves the field at 90,000 while the slider stops at 50,000. The form can then send a different bill chip than the estimate.

**Why it matters:** A homeowner will treat ₹78,000 as an offer. A control that does nothing, and a button named for a calculation it skips, makes the offer feel like a trick.

**Fix:** Name the button for the jump (“Send this estimate”) or make it reveal the result. Write the clamped bill back into the field. Say when the 3 kW floor makes the free-units choice irrelevant. Put state subsidy in the result only when it applies. Send one bill figure to WhatsApp.

**Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

### [P1] The consultation form accepts garbage, then leaves the page

With Name set, the form accepts WhatsApp “hello” and pincode “abc,” any of the five bill chips, and the terms box. Submit opens `wa.me/919216054155` and shows no success state. If that tab is blocked, the page says nothing.

**Why it matters:** This is the high-stakes moment. A bad number means the reply never arrives, and the visitor thinks they already asked.

**Fix:** Constrain the phone to 10 digits and the pincode to 6, with the error under the field. After a successful open, say that WhatsApp opened, and repeat +91 92160 54155 if the window did not.

**Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

### [P1] The savings column is taller than the screen

On desktop the sticky block clips “Turn sunlight into long-term savings.” under the top edge, so the visible line reads as “long term savings.” On a phone, ₹78,000 is on screen while “Standard sizes run from 3 kW…” starts under the bottom edge, and “Calculate my solar savings” starts below an 844px fold.

**Why it matters:** The caveat belongs next to the number. Clipped, the subsidy reads as unconditional.

**Fix:** Let this section scroll as normal content, or split the claim, the calculator, and the disclaimer so each fits one screen, with the disclaimer in the same view as ₹78,000.

**Suggested command:** /impeccable layout

### [P2] The navigation names places that are not places

“Solutions” goes to `#home`, a moment inside the film, not a list of solutions. “About” goes to Why. Process is only in the footer. The header, the hero, and the savings button use four labels for one conversation. The footer lists both info@ellipsesolar.in and sales@ellipsesolar.com.

**Why it matters:** People use the menu to escape the film. Landing mid-animation, unsure whether “Get solar” and “Get a solar consultation” differ, adds effort. Two domains add doubt.

**Fix:** Use one label, “Get a solar consultation.” Point Solutions at a real section. Mark the current chapter. One public email.

**Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify
